# JUKUMU Fund Platform — One-Page Overview

## What it is
JUKUMU Fund is a web platform that supports entrepreneur groups in Tanzania by combining:
- Business training and structured progress tracking
- Group organization and member coordination
- Transparent contribution and activity visibility
- Lightweight decision-making (proposals + voting)
- Capital/investment tracking
- A shared group wallet concept for controlled, auditable group transactions

The platform is designed to help groups build consistent habits (learning, reporting, contributions), make decisions openly, and ultimately grow businesses with accountable financial workflows.

## Who it serves
- **Entrepreneurs (Members):** Build business capacity through training, track progress, and participate in group activities.
- **Group Leadership:** Coordinate members, oversee decisions, and manage group-level actions.
- **Administrators:** Manage onboarding, groups, system settings, and overall oversight.
- **Partners/Investors (future/optional):** View high-level impact and performance reporting.

## Core product areas
### 1) Membership & Profiles
- Member registration and profile management
- Role-based access control (admin vs member; plus group-level leadership roles)

### 2) Groups
- Group membership, roles, and activity overview
- Visibility into group members and leadership

### 3) Training
- Training modules with progress tracking
- Accessible learning resources for members

### 4) Business Reporting
- Business reports to track growth signals (revenue/expenses/metrics)

### 5) Decisions (Proposals + Voting)
- Group proposals created by leadership
- Member voting with transparent vote counts
- A simple structure for making group decisions consistently

### 6) Group Wallet (MVP)
The platform supports a group-level wallet concept where:
- Each group can have a wallet (one per group)
- Members have read-only access to balances and recent transactions
- Leadership can propose outgoing transfers
- Transfers follow an off-chain approval workflow (multi-approval) before execution

This is an MVP designed to provide accountability and visibility for group-managed funds.

## Roles and responsibility model (high-level)
- **Admin:** Platform-wide management (users, groups, settings).
- **Member:** Access to member portal, group participation, training, proposals/voting.
- **Group leadership roles:** Used to gate leadership-only actions such as creating proposals or managing wallet transfers.

## What the platform is intending to do next (direction)
- Strengthen group financial transparency through consistent workflows
- Expand decision-making beyond proposals (e.g., tracked outcomes and approvals)
- Improve reporting and analytics for impact measurement
- Add security and operational tooling (rate limits, audit logs, controlled privileges)

## Success looks like
- Entrepreneurs stay active in learning and reporting
- Groups become better coordinated and more accountable
- Decisions are documented and measurable
- Group finances become more transparent and safer to manage
